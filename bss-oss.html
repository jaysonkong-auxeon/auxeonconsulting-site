Overview

For telecommunications and digital service providers, BSS and OSS platforms decide how quickly new offers reach the market, how accurately customers are billed and how reliably services are delivered.

Auxeon Consulting brings deep, hands-on telecom BSS/OSS experience to every engagement. We help operators, MVNOs and service providers modernise billing, CRM, order management and service assurance — moving toward open, cloud-native architectures while protecting revenue and customer experience during transition.
