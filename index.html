Company Profile

Technology consulting that turns strategy into results.

Auxeon Consulting is a Malaysian technology consulting firm headquartered in Petaling Jaya, Selangor. We help enterprises, service providers and public-sector organisations make confident technology decisions — and see them through to real business outcomes.

Our consultants bring close to two decades of experience across telecommunications, media, insurance and enterprise IT, advising on technology strategy, enterprise and solution architecture, BSS/OSS transformation and large-scale programme delivery.

We start with the business problem, not the product. Every recommendation is grounded in objective assessment, clear business cases and practical roadmaps, with trusted technology partners brought in where they fit best.

Vision

To be Malaysia's most trusted strategic technology advisor, guiding organisations to the right technology decisions for lasting growth.

Mission

Deliver practical, secure and future-ready technology that creates measurable value.
