Introduction

Overview

Growing organisations often run on a patchwork of systems that do not talk to each other, while cyber threats keep growing in number and sophistication.

Auxeon Consulting helps you modernise the business applications that run your organisation and protect them. We select, implement and integrate ERP, CRM and other business apps, automate processes end to end, and put cybersecurity in place from email and endpoint protection to identity, monitoring and incident response.

Auxeon Consulting helps Malaysian companies modernise and protect their business applications, from ERP, CRM and HR platforms, workflow automation and cloud migration to cybersecurity solutions including email and endpoint protection, identity and access management, security monitoring and ISO 27001 readiness.
