Introduction

Overview

Go-live is the beginning, not the end. Systems need monitoring, maintenance, updates and support every day, and internal teams are often stretched thin.

Auxeon Consulting provides managed services that keep infrastructure, applications and platforms healthy around the clock. Working with trusted local partners, we combine proactive monitoring, ITIL-based processes and clear service levels, so issues are fixed quickly and your team can focus on the business.

Auxeon Consulting is an IT managed services provider in Malaysia, working with trusted local partners to deliver 24/7 monitoring, infrastructure and data center operations, application support, service desk and ITIL-based service management against agreed SLAs.
