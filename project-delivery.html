Introduction

Overview

Large technology programmes involve many vendors, stakeholders and dependencies. Without strong leadership, small issues turn into delays, cost overruns and lost confidence.

Auxeon Consulting delivers technology programmes end to end: programme management, solution design, software development and testing. We combine disciplined governance with process redesign and adoption support, so plans turn into measurable change on time.

Auxeon Consulting delivers IT projects and programmes in Malaysia end to end, providing experienced programme managers, PMO services, solution design, software development and testing for BSS, CRM, ERP and infrastructure rollouts, troubled project recovery and digital transformation.
